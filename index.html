Property Management · Landlord Protection

Protect Your Investment.

Most landlords discover problems after the damage is done. I help you prevent them — before a single unauthorized person steps foot in your property.

🚫
Unauthorized Occupants
Tenants subletting to undisclosed parties, allowing unauthorized roommates, or running AirBnB operations without consent are a growing problem in the GTA. I implement lease clauses, move-in inspection protocols, and periodic review systems that catch and prevent unauthorized occupancy before it becomes a costly LTB battle.
🔧
Property Maintenance & Condition
Deferred maintenance destroys asset value. I enforce maintenance clauses, coordinate regular inspections (within legal RTA rights), and connect landlords with a vetted contractor network for fast, cost-controlled repairs — keeping your property in income-generating condition year-round.
📋
Tenant Screening & Qualification
The best way to prevent unauthorized occupants and maintenance issues is rigorous tenant selection. I run comprehensive credit, employment, and reference verification — and know exactly what Ontario law permits landlords to ask and require before signing a lease.
🏛️
LTB Representation & N-Form Guidance
When things go wrong, Ontario's Landlord and Tenant Board process is complex and time-consuming. I guide landlords through the correct N-form applications, hearing preparation, and procedural requirements — maximizing the chance of a successful resolution.
📝
Lease Structuring & Documentation
A standard Ontario Lease Form is the legal minimum — not a landlord's full protection. I add enforceable schedules covering occupancy limits, subletting restrictions, maintenance obligations, and pet conditions that hold up at the LTB.
Why Landlords Choose Jimmy
